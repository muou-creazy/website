About Us | Hebei Solan Technology Co., Ltd

Who We Are

Hebei Solan Technology Co., Ltd. is a professional sports goods supply chain integrator and export enterprise based in Shijiazhuang, China. With 12 years of mature import and export experience, we adopt a self-operated factory + certified cooperative factory dual supply model, focusing on sports protective gear, fitness accessories, outdoor cycling equipment and functional active apparel. We serve global wholesalers, brand sellers and Amazon cross-border merchants with stable and diversified supply capacity.

Different from traditional single suppliers, Solanactive relies on self-owned production workshops for core hot-selling products to guarantee quality and delivery stability. Meanwhile, we cooperate with dozens of certified standardized factories in the sports and outdoor industry for supplementary product sourcing. Through strict factory screening, unified quality standard docking and centralized production supervision, we solve the pain points of scattered suppliers, inconsistent quality and high communication costs for overseas buyers.

Relying on strong supply chain integration capabilities, we not only provide core sports and outdoor products, but also support one-stop mixed procurement and consolidated shipment of multi-category goods. We are deeply experienced in Amazon bulk supply, OEM/ODM customization and DDP door-to-door delivery, providing full-link professional foreign trade solutions for global customers.

Q1: Are you a factory or a trading company?

Q2: Can you support Amazon FBA supply and standard packaging?
A: Yes. Proficient in platform safety standards, FBA labeling, neutral packaging and batch stable delivery.
Q3: Do you provide DDP door-to-door delivery service?
A: Yes, support DDP tax-included delivery for mainstream European and American countries.
